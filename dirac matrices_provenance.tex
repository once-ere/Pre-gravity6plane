Standard mathematics used in the proof of Theorem 3 (not taken from the notebook):


\begin{itemize}
\item Cartan-Dieudonne theorem: every element of the orthogonal group of a non-degenerate real quadratic space of dimension $n$ is a product of at most $n$ reflections $v \mapsto v - 2\frac{(v\cdot n)}{(n\cdot n)}n$.
\item Definition of Pin and Spin groups as groups of products of unit vectors in the Clifford algebra and the twisted adjoint action $\rho(g)v = (-1)^{|g|}gvg^{-1}$; see e.g. H. B. Lawson and M.-L. Michelsohn, Spin Geometry, Princeton University Press (1989), Chapter I.
\item Classification: the real Clifford algebra of signature (4,4) is the full matrix algebra M(16, R), consistent with the computed rank 256.
\end{itemize}
